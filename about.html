Hello! I'm Traditional Techie

Welcome to my little corner of the internet! I'm a passionate content creator who believes in the beauty of Indian traditions and the power of modern technology.

What started as a hobby sharing my grandmother's recipes has grown into a community of 5,000+ subscribers who share the same love for cooking, travel, traditional arts, and technology.

Every video I create is a labor of love — from carefully photographing each dish to editing travel vlogs that capture the essence of India's rich heritage.

When I'm not creating content, you'll find me exploring local markets for ingredients, sketching new Kolam designs, or researching the latest tech gadgets.
